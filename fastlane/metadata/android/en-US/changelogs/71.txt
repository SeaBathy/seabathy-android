High-detail depths outside Australia: US coasts and islands (NOAA, about 3 m), surveyed Canadian coasts (CHS, 10 m), European seas (EMODnet, about 115 m), New Zealand coasts surveyed by lidar (LINZ) and Pacific island reef shallows (Digital Earth Pacific, a satellite estimate). GMRT fills the gaps. The line under the box names the country and region and what it will get, and each layer credits every source in it.
